V21 — AI Reel Quality Judge & Approval Gate

Before publishing, the Judge runs a production preflight across the four cuts. It checks measurable edit signals such as shot variety, opening diversity, pacing, clip quality metadata, rights records and export settings. It is a heuristic production gate — it does not claim to understand every frame or guarantee copyright clearance.
Approval rule: a Reel with missing source/license metadata, fewer than 3 useful shots, repeated opening footage, or an export mismatch is flagged for review. Final posting remains a human decision.

V22 — AI Self-Re-Edit Engine

If the Quality Judge flags a Reel, V22 automatically creates a new edit using a different footage order, opening shot and pacing profile, then runs the Judge again. It does not bypass human approval.
Safety gate: re-editing changes the edit only. It does not create new footage, remove watermarks, or establish copyright permission. Source/license review and your final approval remain required.
